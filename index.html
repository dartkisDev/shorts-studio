Shorts Studio

Vertical videos for our indie games

Shorts Studio is a desktop tool used by our small game studio to turn our own game trailers and gameplay recordings into short vertical videos and publish them to our own channels on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. On Google's sign-in screen the tool appears as “YTShorts GameDev”.

The tool runs entirely on the studio's own computer. It is not a public service and does not have user accounts of its own.

  • It creates short clips from videos we provide.
  • It uploads only the clips we choose, only to accounts we connect ourselves.
  • Access tokens are stored locally and encrypted; nothing is sent to our servers (we have none).
