Shorts Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Shorts Studio (shown as “YTShorts GameDev” on Google's sign-in screen) is a desktop application that our studio uses to create short vertical videos from our own game footage and publish them to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses the application

The application is used only by members of our studio on our own computer. It is not offered as a public service and has no user registration.

Data the application accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with your permission, the scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos you select) and youtube.readonly (to show the name and picture of the connected channel).
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and permission to post videos you select.
  • Instagram: basic profile information (username, profile picture) and permission to publish Reels you select.
  • Video files: game trailers and gameplay recordings that you place in the application's folders.

How the data is used

  • To upload the videos you explicitly choose to the accounts you connected, with the title, caption and privacy setting you set.
  • To display which account is connected.

The application does not read, analyse, modify or delete any other content on your accounts, does not collect analytics and does not show ads.

Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the computer where the application runs, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (readable only by the same Windows user). The application has no servers: no personal data or tokens are transmitted to us or to any third party, except directly to the platform you are uploading to (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram) as part of the upload.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share user data with anyone.

Google API Services

Shorts Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account in the application (which deletes them locally and revokes them with the platform) or delete the application's data folder. You can also revoke access at any time: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok and Instagram — in the apps' settings under connected/authorized apps.
